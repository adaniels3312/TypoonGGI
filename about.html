Tony Typoon

Entrepreneur. Designer. Culture Curator.

Who I Am

I’m Tony Typoon, a creative entrepreneur and graphic designer based in Chattanooga, TN. I’m obsessed with building brands that live at the intersection of street culture, identity, and high-energy design. Every visual, every drop, every concept carries intention and purpose — it’s more than a product; it’s a movement.

Through Typoon Industries and the GGI Collective, I aim to empower creators and hustlers to express themselves authentically, elevate their brands, and leave a mark on culture.

Work With Me

My Journey

  • 2015

    Started experimenting with graphic design and streetwear concepts in my home studio.

  • 2017

    Launched Typoon Industries, focusing on high-energy streetwear and creative branding projects.

  • 2019

    Introduced the GGI Collective — a community-driven streetwear division with purpose and identity.

  • 2022

    Expanded GGI Studios to help other creators and small businesses build culture-first brands.

  • 2026

    Continuing to grow Typoon Industries and push the boundaries of street culture, design, and digital innovation.
